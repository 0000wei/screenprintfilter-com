Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Overview

ScreenPrintFilter.online ("we", "our", or "this service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, use, and protect your information when you use our halftone image processing tool.

2. Information Collection

2.1 Image Processing

All image processing is performed locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. We do not upload your images to our servers, nor do we store, view, or share your images. Your images remain on your device at all times.

2.2 Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Access times and pages visited
  • Language preferences
  • Referring website addresses

3. Use of Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Improve and optimize our services
  • Analyze website usage and trends
  • Prevent technical issues and improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies

ScreenPrintFilter.online may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic via Google Analytics
  • Improve site performance

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features but will not affect the core image processing functionality, which runs entirely client-side.

5. Data Security

Since all image processing is done locally in your browser, your images always remain on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect the technical information we collect, preventing unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics (G-H72N80TEBW) — for understanding website traffic and usage patterns
  • Vercel Inc. — for website hosting and content delivery
  • Google Fonts — for typography

These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices. We do not share your images or personal data with these services.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users of all ages. Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. In the event of significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We recommend regularly reviewing this policy to stay informed.
